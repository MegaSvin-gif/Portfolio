Nouns

Nouns are words that name people, places, things, or ideas. Examples include "dog," "city," and "happiness."

Pronouns

Pronouns are words that take the place of nouns. Examples include "he," "she," "it," and "they."

Verbs

Verbs are words that express actions or states of being. Examples include "run," "think," and "is."
